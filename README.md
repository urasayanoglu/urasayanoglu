# Hi there, I'm Uras Ayanoglu 👋

### Research Engineer | Embedded Systems & IoT | Distributed Real-Time Systems | Autonomous Systems

I'm a **Project Engineer at Turku University of Applied Sciences**, working with the **Autonomous and Intelligent Systems** research group, and a **doctoral researcher at Åbo Akademi University**.

My work sits at the intersection of **embedded systems, distributed computing, edge–cloud architectures, robotics, autonomous systems, and IoT**.

My doctoral research focuses on:

> **Resilient and Trustworthy Architectures for Edge–Cloud Distributed Real-Time Intelligent Systems**

I'm particularly interested in how distributed intelligent systems can remain **reliable, secure, predictable, and fault-tolerant** when computation is spread across embedded devices, edge nodes, and cloud infrastructure.

My background is somewhat unconventional: before moving into engineering and computer science, I studied **Public Finance, International Trade, and Finance** and worked in international trade. That path has given me a multidisciplinary perspective on technology, research, and real-world systems.

## SALAMA Data Collection

One of the systems I'm developing is **SALAMA**, a data-collection environment for managing heterogeneous sensors and ROS 2 data acquisition.

The platform brings together devices such as:

```text
SALAMA
├── RGB Cameras
├── Thermal Cameras
├── PTZ Cameras
├── LiDAR
├── IMU
└── ROS 2 Bag Recording
```

The goal is to make complex multi-sensor research setups easier to **deploy, control, monitor, and record** through a common interface.

Technologies involved include **Python, FastAPI, Docker, ROS 2, Linux, networking, and sensor APIs**.
